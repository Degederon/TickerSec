      How alerts pop up

      The TickerSec pop-up slides in at the bottom-right corner and always shows. Windows notifications also land in the Windows notification center, but Windows hides them when Do Not Disturb or Focus is on, or if TickerSec is switched off in Windows Settings → System → Notifications.
